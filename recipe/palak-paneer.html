Palak Paneer

.cook
📖 https://www.indianhealthyrecipes.com/palak-paneer-recipe-easy-paneer-recipes-step-by-step-pics/

🥘 Ingredients

  • Thai green chilies (deseeded)
    2
  • baby spinach
    4 cups
  • butter
    1 tbsp
  • cinnamon
    1 inch
  • cumin seeds
    1/8 tsp
  • garam masala
    3/4 tsp
  • green cardamoms
    2
  • kasuri methi
    1/2 tsp
  • minced garlic
    3/4 tsp
  • minced ginger
    1/2 tsp
  • minced onion
    3/4 cup
  • oil
    1 tbsp
  • paneer
    150 grams
  • water
    1 cup
  • whole cloves
    2
  • whole unsalted cashews
    8-10

🍳 Cookware

  • pan
  1. 1
    Rinse the baby spinach well, then drain.
    baby spinach: 4 cups
  2. 2
    Heat oil in a pan . Saute the Thai green chilies , whole unsalted cashews and baby spinach for ⏱️ 3-4 minutes until the leaves are completely wilted.
    oil: 1/2 tbsp, Thai green chilies: 2 (deseeded), whole unsalted cashews: 8-10
  3. 3
    Cool the spinach mixture completely, then blend with water into a smooth puree.
    water: 1/4 cup
  4. 4
    Heat butter and oil in the same pan. Once melted, add cinnamon , green cardamoms , whole cloves and cumin seeds . Once the spices sizzle, add minced onion and fry until translucent.
    butter: 1 tbsp, oil: 1/2 tbsp, cinnamon: 1 inch, green cardamoms: 2, whole cloves: 2, cumin seeds: 1/8 tsp, minced onion: 3/4 cup
  5. 5
    Saute minced ginger and minced garlic for ⏱️ 1-2 minutes .
    minced ginger: 1/2 tsp, minced garlic: 3/4 tsp
  6. 6
    Add garam masala and saute until fragrant.
    garam masala: 3/4 tsp
  7. 7
    Pour in water and cover, cooking until the onions are completely soft with some water remaining in the pan.
    water: 3/4 cup
  8. 8
    Lower the heat, then add kasuri methi and the pureed spinach. Mix well and cook until it begins to bubble, about ⏱️ 2-3 minutes . If the curry is too thick, add a few tablespoons of water.
    kasuri methi: 1/2 tsp
  9. 9
    Add paneer and mix well. Once the paneer is warm, serve with naan, roti or basmati rice.
    paneer: 150 grams
